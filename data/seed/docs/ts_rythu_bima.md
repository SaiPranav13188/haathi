# Rythu Bima – Farmers Group Life Insurance (Telangana)
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://agri.telangana.gov.in. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
Rythu Bima is a group life insurance scheme of the Government of Telangana. It gives financial security to the family of a farmer who dies due to any cause, including natural death.

## 2. Eligibility
Farmers who own agricultural land (pattadars) in Telangana are covered.
The farmer must be aged between 18 and 59 years at the time of enrolment.
The farmer must enrol by giving the details of a nominee.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
An insurance cover of ₹5,00,000 is paid to the nominee within 10 days of the death of the farmer.
The entire premium is paid by the Government of Telangana; the farmer pays nothing.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Pattadar passbook (land record)
- Nominee's Aadhaar card
- Bank account passbook of the nominee

## 5. How to apply
Submit the enrolment form with nominee details to the Agriculture Extension Officer (AEO) of your cluster. Renewal happens every year in August.
