# Aasara Pensions – Old Age (Telangana)

## 1. About the scheme
Aasara Pensions is the social security pension scheme of the Government of Telangana for the elderly, widows, persons with disabilities and other vulnerable groups. This summary covers the old age pension.

## 2. Eligibility
Old age pension is given to persons who have completed 57 years of age and reside in Telangana.
The annual family income should not exceed ₹1,50,000 in rural areas.
The annual family income should not exceed ₹2,00,000 in urban areas.
Persons who are government employees or pensioners are not eligible.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
A monthly pension of ₹2,016 is paid to each eligible old age pensioner.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Age proof
- Income certificate
- Bank account passbook or post office account
- Ration card

## 5. How to apply
Apply at the Gram Panchayat office (rural) or the Municipal office (urban). Applications are verified by the Mandal Parishad Development Officer or Municipal Commissioner.
