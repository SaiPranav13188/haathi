# Atal Pension Yojana (APY)
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://www.npscra.nsdl.co.in. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
Atal Pension Yojana is a pension scheme for citizens, focused on workers in the unorganised sector.

## 2. Eligibility
Any citizen of India between 18 and 40 years of age can join.
The subscriber must have a savings bank account.
From 1 October 2022, persons who are or have been income tax payers are not eligible to join.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
A guaranteed monthly pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 after the age of 60, depending on the contribution.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Bank account passbook

## 5. How to apply
Fill the APY registration form at your bank or post office where you hold a savings account, or enrol through net banking.
