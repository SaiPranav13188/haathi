# Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Caste Students (Central)
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://scholarships.gov.in. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
This centrally sponsored scholarship supports Scheduled Caste students studying at the post-matriculation or post-secondary stage.

## 2. Eligibility
The student must belong to a Scheduled Caste.
The student must be studying in Class 11 or a higher course after passing the matriculation (Class 10) examination.
The annual income of the parents or guardians should not exceed ₹2,50,000.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Compulsory non-refundable fees paid to the institution, and an academic allowance of ₹2,500 to ₹13,500 per year depending on the course.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Caste certificate
- Income certificate
- Previous year marks memo
- Bank account passbook

## 5. How to apply
Apply on the National Scholarship Portal or the state scholarship portal (Telangana ePASS / AP Jnanabhumi) as notified by your state.
