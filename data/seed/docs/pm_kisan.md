# PM-KISAN – Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://pmkisan.gov.in. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
PM-KISAN is a Central Sector scheme of the Government of India that gives income support to all landholding farmer families in the country.

## 2. Eligibility
All landholding farmer families, who have cultivable land in their names, are eligible for the benefit.
The bank account of the farmer must be seeded with Aadhaar, and e-KYC must be completed.
Families in which any member paid income tax in the last assessment year are excluded.
Institutional landholders, serving or retired government employees (except Group D / MTS), and pensioners receiving ₹10,000 or more per month are also excluded.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Income support of ₹6,000 per year is paid in three equal instalments of ₹2,000 every four months, directly into the bank account of the farmer.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Land record (pattadar passbook)
- Bank account passbook (Aadhaar-seeded)

## 5. How to apply
Register through the Farmers Corner on the PM-KISAN portal, at a Common Service Centre (CSC), or with the local revenue officer. Complete e-KYC with OTP or biometrics.
