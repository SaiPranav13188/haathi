# Gruha Jyothi (Telangana)
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://www.tgsouthernpower.org. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

## 1. About the scheme
Gruha Jyothi is one of the Telangana government's guarantees. It provides free electricity to domestic households for monthly consumption up to 200 units.

## 2. Eligibility
Domestic households in Telangana holding a white ration card (food security card) are eligible.
The household must have applied under Praja Palana and must have a domestic electricity connection.
Households consuming more than 200 units in a month pay the full bill for that month.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Free electricity for up to 200 units of consumption per month; the bill is shown as zero for eligible households.

## 4. Documents required
- White ration card (food security card)
- Aadhaar card
- Electricity service connection number

## 5. How to apply
Submit the Praja Palana application at the Gram Sabha or ward office. Link your Aadhaar and ration card to your electricity service number at the electricity office.
