# Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://www.jansuraksha.gov.in. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
PMJJBY is a one-year renewable life insurance scheme that covers death due to any reason.

## 2. Eligibility
Persons aged 18 to 50 years having a savings bank account can join.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Life cover of ₹2,00,000 for a premium of ₹436 per year, auto-debited from the bank account.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Bank account passbook

## 5. How to apply
Enrol at your bank branch, through net banking, or on the Jan Suraksha portal.
