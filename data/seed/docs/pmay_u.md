# PMAY-U 2.0 – Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Urban)
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://pmay-urban.gov.in. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
PMAY-U 2.0 helps urban poor and middle-income families to build, buy or rent a pucca house in urban areas.

## 2. Eligibility
The scheme is for families living in urban areas.
The family must not own a pucca house anywhere in India.
Families with annual income up to ₹9,00,000 are eligible, covering EWS (up to ₹3 lakh), LIG (₹3 to 6 lakh) and MIG (₹6 to 9 lakh) categories.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Central assistance of ₹2,50,000 per unit for Beneficiary Led Construction and Affordable Housing in Partnership, along with state share.
An interest subsidy on home loans for eligible EWS, LIG and MIG families.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Income certificate
- Bank account passbook
- Land document (for Beneficiary Led Construction)

## 5. How to apply
Apply online on the PMAY-U 2.0 portal or at your Urban Local Body (municipality) office.
