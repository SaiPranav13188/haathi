# Rythu Bharosa (Telangana)

## 1. About the scheme
Rythu Bharosa is the investment support scheme of the Government of Telangana for farmers. It replaced the earlier Rythu Bandhu scheme from January 2025 and helps farmers meet the cost of seeds, fertiliser and labour before each crop season.

## 2. Eligibility
The scheme is meant for farmers who hold cultivable agricultural land in the State of Telangana.
All pattadar farmers owning cultivable agricultural land in Telangana are eligible for investment support.
Land that is not fit for cultivation, such as land used for real estate, mining, roads or non-agricultural purposes, is excluded.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Investment support of ₹12,000 per acre per year is paid in two instalments of ₹6,000 per acre, one for the Vanakalam (Kharif) season and one for the Yasangi (Rabi) season.
The amount is transferred directly to the farmer's bank account.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Pattadar passbook (land record)
- Bank account passbook

## 5. How to apply
Farmers already registered in the land records (Dharani / Bhu Bharathi) receive the support automatically. New pattadars should contact the local Agriculture Extension Officer (AEO) or Mandal Agriculture Officer with their pattadar passbook and bank details.
