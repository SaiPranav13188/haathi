# NTR Bharosa Pensions – Old Age (Andhra Pradesh)

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
NTR Bharosa is the social security pension scheme of the Government of Andhra Pradesh. This summary covers the old age pension.

## 2. Eligibility
Old age pension is given to persons who have completed 60 years of age and reside in Andhra Pradesh.
The family must hold a white rice card.
Persons who are government employees or pensioners are not eligible.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
A monthly pension of ₹4,000 is delivered at the doorstep by the Village / Ward Secretariat staff on the first day of every month.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Age proof
- White rice card
- Bank account passbook

## 5. How to apply
Apply at your Village / Ward Secretariat.
