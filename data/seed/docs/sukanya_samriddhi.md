# Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://www.nsiindia.gov.in. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana is a small savings scheme for the education and marriage of a girl child, with a high interest rate and tax benefits.

## 2. Eligibility
A parent or legal guardian can open the account in the name of a girl child who is below 10 years of age.
A maximum of two accounts can be opened per family.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Minimum deposit of ₹250 and maximum deposit of ₹1,50,000 per financial year, with interest set by the government every quarter.
Deposits qualify for tax deduction under Section 80C.

## 4. Documents required
- Birth certificate of the girl child
- Aadhaar card of the parent
- Address proof

## 5. How to apply
Open the account at any post office or authorised bank branch.
