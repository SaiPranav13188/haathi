# Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
IGNOAPS is a component of the National Social Assistance Programme that provides a monthly pension to elderly persons from poor households.

## 2. Eligibility
The applicant must be aged 60 years or above.
The applicant must belong to a household below the poverty line (BPL).

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Central assistance of ₹200 per month for persons aged 60 to 79 years and ₹500 per month for persons aged 80 years and above. States add their own top-up, and in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh it is paid together with the state pension.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Age proof
- BPL ration card
- Bank account passbook

## 5. How to apply
Apply at the Gram Panchayat or Municipal office through the state's social security pension system.
