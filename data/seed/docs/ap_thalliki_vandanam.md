# Thalliki Vandanam (Andhra Pradesh)
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://gsws-nbm.ap.gov.in. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
Thalliki Vandanam gives yearly financial support to mothers or guardians of school-going children, to encourage education.

## 2. Eligibility
The mother or guardian must reside in Andhra Pradesh and have a child studying in Class 1 to Class 12.
The family must hold a white rice card.
The child must have at least 75% attendance in school.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Financial assistance of ₹15,000 per year for each school-going child, credited to the mother's bank account.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card of mother and child
- White rice card
- School study certificate
- Bank account passbook of the mother

## 5. How to apply
Beneficiaries are identified through school records. Contact your Village / Ward Secretariat if your name is missing.
