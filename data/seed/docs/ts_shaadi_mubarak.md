# Shaadi Mubarak (Telangana)
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
Shaadi Mubarak gives one-time financial assistance to minority families at the time of a daughter's marriage.

## 2. Eligibility
The bride's family must belong to a minority community and reside in Telangana.
The bride must have completed 18 years of age at the time of marriage.
The combined annual income of the parents should not exceed ₹1,50,000 in rural areas.
The combined annual income of the parents should not exceed ₹2,00,000 in urban areas.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
One-time financial assistance of ₹1,00,116 is credited to the bank account of the bride's mother.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card of bride, groom and mother
- Community certificate
- Income certificate
- Age proof of the bride
- Bank account passbook of the mother

## 5. How to apply
Apply online on the Telangana ePASS portal (Kalyana Lakshmi / Shaadi Mubarak section) and submit printed copies at the MRO / Tahsildar office for verification.
