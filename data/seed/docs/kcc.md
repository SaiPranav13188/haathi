# Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/kcc. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
The Kisan Credit Card scheme gives farmers timely and affordable short-term credit for cultivation, post-harvest expenses and allied activities like dairy and fisheries.

## 2. Eligibility
Farmers, tenant farmers, oral lessees and sharecroppers engaged in cultivation are eligible.
The borrower should be between 18 and 75 years of age; a co-borrower is required if the farmer is above 60 years.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Short-term loans up to ₹3,00,000 at 7% interest, with an additional 3% prompt repayment incentive, making the effective rate 4% per year.
Collateral-free loans up to ₹2,00,000.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Land record or tenancy proof
- Passport size photograph
- Bank account passbook

## 5. How to apply
Apply at any commercial bank, regional rural bank or cooperative bank branch, or through the PM-KISAN portal's KCC form.
