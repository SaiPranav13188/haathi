# Mahalakshmi – Free Bus Travel for Women (Telangana)
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://www.tgsrtc.telangana.gov.in. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
Under the Mahalakshmi guarantee, women, girls and transgender persons who are residents of Telangana can travel free of cost in TGSRTC buses within the state.

## 2. Eligibility
Free travel is available to women, girls and transgender persons residing in Telangana.
Travel must be within the borders of Telangana in Palle Velugu, Express, City Ordinary and Metro Express buses.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Zero-fare ticket (free travel) in eligible TGSRTC buses within Telangana. There is no limit on the number of trips.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card or any government photo ID showing a Telangana address

## 5. How to apply
No application is needed. Show your Aadhaar card or Telangana address proof to the conductor to get a zero-fare ticket.
