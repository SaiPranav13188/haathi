# Annadata Sukhibhava (Andhra Pradesh)

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
Annadata Sukhibhava is the investment support scheme of the Government of Andhra Pradesh for farmers, implemented together with PM-KISAN.

## 2. Eligibility
Farmer families who own cultivable agricultural land in Andhra Pradesh are eligible.
Tenant farmers holding a Crop Cultivator Rights Card (CCRC) are also eligible.
Families in which any member is an income tax payer are excluded.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Financial assistance of ₹20,000 per year per farmer family, including ₹6,000 from PM-KISAN, paid in instalments.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Land record (pattadar passbook) or CCRC card
- Bank account passbook

## 5. How to apply
Registration is done through the Rythu Seva Kendram (RSK) in your village with e-KYC.
