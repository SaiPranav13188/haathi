# PM Shram Yogi Maan-dhan (PM-SYM)
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://maandhan.in. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
PM-SYM is a voluntary and contributory pension scheme for unorganised workers such as street vendors, domestic workers, construction workers and agricultural labourers.

## 2. Eligibility
The applicant must be an unorganised worker aged between 18 and 40 years.
The monthly income of the worker must be ₹15,000 or less.
The worker should not be a member of EPFO, ESIC or NPS, and should not be an income tax payer.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
An assured monthly pension of ₹3,000 after the age of 60. The government contributes an amount equal to the worker's contribution.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Savings bank account or Jan Dhan account passbook

## 5. How to apply
Enrol at a Common Service Centre (CSC) or on the Maandhan portal with your Aadhaar and bank details.
