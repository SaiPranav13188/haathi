# Rajiv Aarogyasri Health Insurance (Telangana)
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://www.aarogyasri.telangana.gov.in. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
Rajiv Aarogyasri is the health insurance scheme of the Government of Telangana. It provides cashless treatment for serious illnesses in government and network private hospitals.

## 2. Eligibility
Families in Telangana holding a white ration card (food security card) are eligible.
All members of the family listed on the ration card are covered.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Cashless treatment up to ₹10,00,000 per family per year for listed procedures, including surgeries, therapies and follow-up care.
There is no premium to be paid by the beneficiary.

## 4. Documents required
- White ration card (food security card)
- Aadhaar card
- Aarogyasri health card, if issued

## 5. How to apply
Visit the Aarogyamithra help desk at any network hospital with your white ration card and Aadhaar card. No prior application is needed.
