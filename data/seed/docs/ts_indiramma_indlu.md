# Indiramma Indlu – Housing (Telangana)

## 1. About the scheme
Indiramma Indlu is the housing scheme of the Government of Telangana. It gives financial assistance to poor families to build their own pucca house.

## 2. Eligibility
The family must reside in Telangana and hold a white ration card.
The family must not own a pucca house anywhere.
Priority is given to families who own a house site (plot) and to women-headed households.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Financial assistance of ₹5,00,000 is given to build a house, released in stages as construction progresses.
The house is sanctioned in the name of the woman of the family.

## 4. Documents required
- White ration card (food security card)
- Aadhaar card
- House site (plot) document, if available
- Bank account passbook

## 5. How to apply
Apply through the Praja Palana application or the Indiramma Indlu app survey conducted in your village or ward. Beneficiary lists are approved by the Gram Sabha or Ward Sabha.
