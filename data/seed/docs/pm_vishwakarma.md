# PM Vishwakarma

## 1. About the scheme
PM Vishwakarma supports traditional artisans and craftspeople working with their hands and tools, such as carpenters, blacksmiths, potters, goldsmiths, tailors, cobblers and weavers of mats and baskets.

## 2. Eligibility
An artisan or craftsperson working in one of the 18 listed family-based trades is eligible.
The applicant must be at least 18 years old on the date of registration.
Persons in government service and their family members are not eligible.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Recognition through a PM Vishwakarma certificate and ID card, a toolkit incentive of ₹15,000, skill training with a stipend of ₹500 per day, and collateral-free loans of ₹1,00,000 and ₹2,00,000 at a concessional interest rate of 5%.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Bank account passbook
- Ration card

## 5. How to apply
Register at a Common Service Centre (CSC) with Aadhaar-based biometric verification. The application is verified by the Gram Panchayat or Urban Local Body.
