# PMFBY – Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://pmfby.gov.in. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
PMFBY provides crop insurance to farmers against loss of crop yield due to natural calamities, pests and diseases.

## 2. Eligibility
All farmers including sharecroppers and tenant farmers growing notified crops in notified areas are eligible for coverage.
Enrolment is voluntary for all farmers.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
The farmer pays a maximum premium of 2% of the sum insured for Kharif crops, 1.5% for Rabi crops and 5% for commercial and horticultural crops. The balance premium is shared by the Central and State Governments.
Claims are paid directly into the farmer's bank account.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Land record or tenancy agreement
- Sowing certificate
- Bank account passbook

## 5. How to apply
Enrol through your bank, a Common Service Centre (CSC), an insurance company agent, or the PMFBY portal before the cut-off date for the season.
