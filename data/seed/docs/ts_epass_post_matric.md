# Post-Matric Scholarship – ePASS (Telangana)
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

## 1. About the scheme
The Telangana ePASS post-matric scholarship pays tuition fee reimbursement and a maintenance allowance to students from weaker sections studying in Intermediate, degree, professional and post-graduate courses.

## 2. Eligibility
The student must be a resident of Telangana studying in Intermediate (Class 11) or a higher course.
Students from Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe or Minority families are eligible if the parents' annual income does not exceed ₹2,00,000.
Students from Backward Class or Economically Backward Class families are eligible if the parents' annual income does not exceed ₹1,50,000 in rural areas.
For Backward Class or Economically Backward Class families in urban areas, the parents' annual income should not exceed ₹2,00,000.
Since G.O.Ms.No.18 of 29-09-2022, minority students in rural areas have the same ₹2,00,000 income limit as SC and ST students.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Reimbursement of tuition fee (RTF) paid directly to the college, and a maintenance fee (MTF) paid to the student's bank account.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Caste certificate
- Income certificate
- Previous year marks memo
- Bank account passbook of the student

## 5. How to apply
Register on the Telangana ePASS portal every academic year, upload documents, and submit the printout with biometric verification at your college.
