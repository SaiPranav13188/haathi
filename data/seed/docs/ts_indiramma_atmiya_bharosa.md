# Indiramma Atmiya Bharosa (Telangana)

## 1. About the scheme
Indiramma Atmiya Bharosa provides yearly financial support to landless agricultural labour families in Telangana, who are not covered by land-based farmer schemes.

## 2. Eligibility
The scheme is for landless agricultural labour families residing in Telangana.
The family must not own any agricultural land.
The family should have worked under the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme (MGNREGS), with at least 20 days of work in the reference year.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Financial assistance of ₹12,000 per year is paid to each eligible landless agricultural labour family, in two instalments of ₹6,000.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- MGNREGS job card
- Bank account passbook
- Ration card

## 5. How to apply
Beneficiaries are identified through Gram Sabhas using MGNREGS job card records. Families who are left out can submit an application at the Gram Panchayat or Mandal office.
