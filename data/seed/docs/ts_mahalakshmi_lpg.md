# Mahalakshmi – LPG Cylinder at ₹500 (Telangana)
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://www.telangana.gov.in. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
Under the Mahalakshmi guarantee, eligible households in Telangana get domestic LPG cylinders at a subsidised price of ₹500.

## 2. Eligibility
Households in Telangana holding a white ration card are eligible.
The household must already have a domestic LPG connection in the name of a woman of the family.
The household must have applied under Praja Palana.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
The household pays the full price of the cylinder and the difference above ₹500 is credited back to the bank account.

## 4. Documents required
- White ration card (food security card)
- Aadhaar card
- LPG consumer number
- Bank account passbook

## 5. How to apply
Submit the Praja Palana application and link your LPG consumer number, Aadhaar and bank account at your gas agency.
