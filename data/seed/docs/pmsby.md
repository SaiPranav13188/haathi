# Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://www.jansuraksha.gov.in. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
PMSBY is a one-year renewable accident insurance scheme covering death or disability due to an accident.

## 2. Eligibility
Persons aged 18 to 70 years having a savings bank account can join.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Cover of ₹2,00,000 for accidental death or full disability and ₹1,00,000 for partial disability, for a premium of ₹20 per year.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Bank account passbook

## 5. How to apply
Enrol at your bank branch, through net banking, or on the Jan Suraksha portal.
