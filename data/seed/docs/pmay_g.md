# PMAY-G – Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin)
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://pmayg.nic.in. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
PMAY-G provides financial assistance to houseless families and families living in kutcha or dilapidated houses in rural areas to build a pucca house.

## 2. Eligibility
The scheme covers houseless households and households living in kutcha houses in rural areas.
The household must not own a pucca house.
Beneficiaries are selected from the Awaas+ survey list and verified by the Gram Sabha.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
Assistance of ₹1,20,000 in plain areas and ₹1,30,000 in hilly and difficult areas for building a house.
Additional support for toilet construction and 90-95 days of unskilled wages under MGNREGS.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Job card under MGNREGS
- Bank account passbook

## 5. How to apply
Contact your Gram Panchayat. Eligible families are identified through the Awaas+ survey and approved by the Gram Sabha.
