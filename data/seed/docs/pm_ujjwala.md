# PM Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)
> Haathi seed summary of the official scheme guidelines. Official source: https://www.pmuy.gov.in. Not an official government document; always verify on the official portal.

<!-- page 1 -->
## 1. About the scheme
PM Ujjwala Yojana provides deposit-free LPG connections to women from poor households, for clean cooking fuel.

## 2. Eligibility
The applicant must be a woman who has completed 18 years of age.
The applicant must belong to a poor household, such as a household holding a BPL or priority ration card.
There should not be any other LPG connection in the same household.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
A deposit-free LPG connection with the first refill and stove free of cost, and a targeted subsidy on refills.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Ration card
- Bank account passbook
- Passport size photograph

## 5. How to apply
Apply online on the PMUY portal or visit the nearest LPG distributor with your documents.
