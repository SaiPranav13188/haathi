# Kalyana Lakshmi (Telangana)

## 1. About the scheme
Kalyana Lakshmi gives one-time financial assistance to families at the time of a daughter's marriage, to reduce the burden of marriage expenses and to prevent child marriage.

## 2. Eligibility
The bride's family must belong to the Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, Backward Classes or Economically Backward Classes and reside in Telangana.
The bride must have completed 18 years of age at the time of marriage.
The combined annual income of the parents should not exceed ₹1,50,000 in rural areas.
The combined annual income of the parents should not exceed ₹2,00,000 in urban areas.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Benefits
One-time financial assistance of ₹1,00,116 is credited to the bank account of the bride's mother.

## 4. Documents required
- Aadhaar card of bride, groom and mother
- Caste certificate
- Income certificate
- Age proof of the bride
- Marriage invitation or certificate
- Bank account passbook of the mother

## 5. How to apply
Apply online on the Telangana ePASS portal (Kalyana Lakshmi / Shaadi Mubarak section) after the marriage date is fixed, and submit printed copies at the MRO / Tahsildar office for verification.
